# Research

## 1. Nonlocal Metamaterials

Our research investigates metamaterials with long-range interactions beyond conventional nearest-neighbor coupling.

Topics include:
- Long-range interactions
- Beyond-nearest-neighbor coupling
- Nonlocal continuum mechanics
- Dispersion engineering
- Roton-like dispersion
- Van Hove singularities


## 2. Phononic Metamaterials

We investigate mechanical and acoustic wave propagation in architected materials.

Topics include:
- Phononic crystals
- Wave propagation
- Band structures
- Topological mechanics
- Nonlinear waves


## 3. Thermal Metamaterials

We investigate unconventional heat transport using architected materials.

Topics include:
- Nonlocal thermal transport
- Anomalous thermal diffusion
- Thermal metamaterials
- Thermal management
- Chip cooling


## 4. Active Metamaterials

We develop programmable metamaterials using active components and feedback control.

Topics include:
- Piezoelectric actuation
- Programmable interactions
- Time-varying coupling
- Non-reciprocal systems
- Active wave control


## 5. Multiphysics Sensing and Manipulation

We investigate coupled physical fields for sensing, detection, and manipulation at small scales.

Topics include:
- Multiphysics sensing
- On-chip sensing
- Micro/nano manipulation
- Wave-based detection
